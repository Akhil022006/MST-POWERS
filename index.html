About MST Powers

Located in the heart of Kakinada, MST Powers is your premium partner for uninterrupted operations. We serve as the authorised sales and service partner for **Genesis Poweronics India Pvt. Ltd.**, bringing you original equipment manufacturer (OEM) Ashok Leyland diesel generators.

Whether it's a hospital safeguarding lives, a manufacturing plant running complex processes, or a stadium lighting up an event, we deliver standby power that never compromises on quality, safety, or efficiency.

Why Choose Our Generators?

Our Ashok Leyland powered diesel generators represent a brilliant feat of power engineering. Highly intelligent, durable, and fuel-efficient.

  • CPCB IV+ Eco-Compliant: Fully compliant with the Central Pollution Control Board's newest and most stringent emission limits.
  • Intelligent AMF Support: Advanced digital controllers with auto-start, engine protection diagnostics, and smart load transfer indicators.
  • Superb Fuel Economy: Ashok Leyland's high-torque engines deliver unmatched fuel-to-power efficiency, lowering operating costs.
  • Acoustic Canopy Protection: Weatherproof, powder-coated acoustic enclosures designed to restrict sound levels as per government norms.
